      Recommendation confidence thresholds

      Below the lower threshold ShiftWise only asks for more evidence. Between the two it says “I think you should…”. Above the upper it says “You must…”. It never invents a recommendation it can't defend.

      Recommend (“I think you should”) 60%
      Below 60% confidence, the platform's next best action is always gather more evidence: e.g. request a field check.
      Mandate (“You must”) 90%
      At or above 90%, the recommendation is presented as required, still approved and executed by a person.
      Evidence staleness limit 30 min
      Feeds older than this can't be cited as evidence without a confirmation step.

      Ranking weights

      How ShiftWise orders the queue once safety conditions are cleared. Safety is never weighted or traded against production, and a failed enabling system (network, power) always comes first. Weight changes need approval from the site's operations owner.

      Critical path (downstream blocked)30
      Time sensitivity25
      Feasibility & effort15
      Confidence in the evidence15
      Protects the week / month15
      Unresolved items also gain urgency with age, and confidence has to build (red → amber → green) before an action is recommended, so the operator isn't re-prompted every 30 seconds.
